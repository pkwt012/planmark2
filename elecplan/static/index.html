    How to mark a plan

    • Click a symbol, then click the sheet to place it. Click the symbol again to put the tool away.
    • Drag a mark to move it. Drag the amber handle to rotate the whole symbol. Hold Shift to snap to 15°.
    • A symbol is clicked in its center, about 65% of the box. The drawing and the amber handle stay full size. Click the same spot again to select the next symbol when those centers still overlap.
    • Letters turn with the symbol. Drag the dividers to resize the symbol list and the tools.
    • Click a category name to show or hide its symbols.
    • Note places a short label on the sheet. Click where it should sit, then type it. Drag it off to the side. A note is text, so it is left out of the symbol legend and the counts.
    • Arrow points at the work. Click the first point, then click where the arrow points. Esc cancels that arrow.
    • Dashed line connects lights. Click the first point, then the next. An end snapped to a symbol stays there when the symbol moves. A blue end is attached. Drag that end away, or drag the line, to disconnect it. Esc cancels the segment.
    • Where a dashed line or a run crosses another, the one drawn later hops over it with a small jump. The hop moves when you move either line.
    • Shift-click two to five nearby switches, then Group switches. They pack tightly, about 0.6 of a symbol apart, and each switch still takes its own dashed line. Dragging one moves the whole box. Ungroup separates them.
    • Light layout draws a construction rectangle. Click one corner, then the opposite corner. Hold Shift for a square. No symbols are placed. Click a symbol, then click the line to put it on the rectangle.
    • White box covers the plan with an opaque white rectangle. Click one corner, then the opposite corner. Hold Shift for a square. Drag the rectangle to move it, or drag an amber corner to resize it. Export paints these first, so they cover the original sheet and the symbols stay on top. Export final erases the text and scanned image under each box, so it cannot be copied or uncovered. Export all layers paints the box in its layer instead, so turning that layer off shows the plan again. The original PDF is not changed.
    • Snap to plan lines pulls a light onto a strong line in the sheet, centered on that line. An outlet or a switch sits on the open side of that line, against the wall. The outlet lines run out from the wall. A switch turns to follow the wall. Floor plugs, ceiling lights, and any mark set to Free place ignore it. Hold Shift to place or drag one symbol without snapping.
    • Layers are Power, Lighting, Low-volt, Circuitry, or a name you type. Uncheck a layer to hide it on the sheet, in the counts, and in the legend. When only one layer is checked, new symbols, dashed lines, and other marks go on that layer. Export all layers writes each layer as a group in the PDF. A white box in that export stays with its layer.
    • Circuit is an optional label. Circuits in the header shows it next to the symbol. Add schedule sheet lists each circuit, its description, the count, and notes. A dashed line or run takes the circuit of a symbol it snaps to when it does not already have one.
    • Under cabinet 120V and LED tape light are runs. Click one end, then the other. The words sit under the line. Esc cancels that run.
    • The symbol list also has step, art, shop, vanity, and chandelier lights, 4 in and 6 in cans, sensor and fan controls, 20A and half-hot outlets, generator inlets, and equipment such as a range hood, disposal, water heater, furnace, pumps, a hardwired EV charger, transfer switches, a subpanel, a junction box, and a gate.
    • Recent symbols stay at the top of the list, the last few you picked.
    • Select an outlet and type a height, such as 48 or 18. A plain number is inches. It prints under the outlet. Counts and the legend list each height on its own. Clear the field to leave the height off. The next outlet you place keeps that height until you change it.
    • Apply size to all copies the selected symbol’s size onto the other marks of that type. This sheet only is the usual choice. All sheets covers the whole plan.
    • Counts lists each symbol that is on the plan. Add counts sheet puts that list on a new page.
    • Drop legend places a key of the symbols placed on the plan. Drag it where the crew can read it. The key follows those symbols as you add or delete them. A hidden layer stays off it.
    • Legend in the header adds a sheet of those same symbols, with the name and a short description. The sheet stays as it was when you added it.
    • Calibrate on a known dimension (the sample has a 10 ft line) so size can be read in feet.
    • Export to revise writes a marked PDF you can open again and keep editing. Export final draws the marks into a PDF to send out. Opening the final file does not bring the marks back for editing. A recovery copy is written just before either export.
    • Marks are saved in this browser, and about every 75 seconds as a JSON file in the planmarks-recovery folder next to the program (or the project folder when you run from source). Closing the tab writes that file too. Opening the same plan offers to restore a newer recovery. Load can open that JSON as well. Sheets you add are kept in that folder too, and opening the original PDF asks whether to bring them back. Files in that folder that sit unused for 90 days are removed when the program starts.

    Shortcuts

    • Wheel zoom
    • Right-drag or Space-drag pan
    • R / Shift+R rotate 15°
    • Delete remove the selected mark
    • Ctrl+D duplicate · Alt-drag also duplicates
    • Arrows nudge · Shift nudges farther
    • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo
    • Ctrl+S save marks · Ctrl+O open PDF
    • Esc cancel calibrate, tool, or selection
